Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 May 2026

Catholic Prayer Book / Modlitewnik Katolicki is a Catholic prayer application that provides prayers, Bible verses, the Daily Gospel, Saint of the Day, favourites, recent prayers, reader settings and a Pompeian Novena progress tracker.

This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information We Collect

The app does not require account registration and does not ask users to provide personal details such as name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment information.

The app stores some information locally on the user’s device to make the app work properly, including:

  • selected app language;
  • selected reader font, theme and text size;
  • favourite prayers;
  • recently opened prayers;
  • last opened prayer;
  • onboarding status;
  • cached Daily Gospel and Saint of the Day content;
  • Pompeian Novena progress;
  • Pompeian Novena intention entered by the user.

This information is stored locally on the device and is not intentionally sent to the developer.

Online Features

Some features use an internet connection to refresh religious content, such as the Daily Gospel and Saint of the Day. The app may connect to external content sources such as Catholic websites and Vatican News to retrieve publicly available religious content.

The app may also use Google services, including Google Translate, to translate some text content when required by the selected language.

When the app connects to external services, those services may automatically receive standard technical information such as IP address, device request information and request time. This is handled by those third-party services according to their own privacy policies.

Sharing and Clipboard

The app allows users to copy prayer text, Bible verses or saint content to the clipboard.

The app may also allow users to share selected text or generated saint images using the device’s standard sharing options. Sharing only happens when the user chooses to share content. The selected content is then handled by the app or service chosen by the user.

How We Use Information

Local app data is used only to:

  • remember user preferences;
  • keep favourites and recent prayers;
  • restore reading progress;
  • save Pompeian Novena progress and intention;
  • cache Daily Gospel and Saint of the Day content;
  • improve the user experience inside the app.

The developer does not sell personal data.

Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services or libraries for app functionality, including:

  • Google Fonts, for displaying fonts;
  • Google Translate / Google services, for translation features;
  • external Catholic content websites, for Daily Gospel and Saint of the Day content;
  • the device sharing system, when the user chooses to share content.

These services may process technical data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored by the app remains on the user’s device until the user deletes it, clears the app data or uninstalls the app.

Users can remove local data by:

  • clearing the app storage in Android settings; or
  • uninstalling the app.

Because the app does not create user accounts, there is no remote account data to delete.

Security

The app uses HTTPS connections where supported by external services. Local data is stored on the user’s device using standard Android app storage mechanisms.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, but the app is designed to avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.

Children’s Privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
